# Contributing

MaterialScope is licensed under AGPL-3.0-only and may also be offered
under a separate proprietary/commercial license (dual licensing; see
[COMMERCIAL-LICENSING.md](COMMERCIAL-LICENSING.md)).

## Contribution policy

- Contributions are welcome only under this documented policy.
- Because the project intends to preserve the ability to offer
  proprietary dual licensing, a proper contributor license agreement
  (CLA) or equivalent contributor-rights mechanism is required before
  external code can be merged.
- Until a CLA or equivalent contributor-rights mechanism is adopted, do
  not merge external code contributions.
- Issues, bug reports, discussions, and suggestions remain welcome via
  the repository's issue tracker.

No contributor agreement exists yet. Submitting a contribution under
AGPL-3.0-only does not, by itself, grant the project owner the right to
relicense that contribution under proprietary terms.
